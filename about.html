About Pitzy

We help founders become investor-ready.

Raising funds is about more than having a great startup. You need a clear story, a strong pitch, the right investors, and a consistent way to reach them.

Pitzy brings all of these together in one focused fundraising process.

We work with Pre-Seed and Seed founders to build investor-ready pitch decks, identify relevant investors, and manage personalised outreach and follow-ups.

Why Pitzy?

"Weeks spent figuring out fundraising alone."

Founders were creating pitch decks, searching for investors, writing outreach messages, and following up — without knowing whether they were reaching the right people.

"The problem wasn't always the startup."

Often, it was the fundraising process around it. Pitzy was built to make that process simpler, more structured, and more focused.

What we do.

Three parts of the raise, run as one process.

01

Pitch Decks

We turn your startup, vision, and traction into a clear investor story through a professionally designed pitch deck.

02

Investor Research

We research and identify investors that match your startup's stage, sector, funding requirements, and overall profile.

03

Investor Outreach

We create personalised outreach and handle follow-ups to help you connect with relevant investors and start meaningful conversations.

Our approach.

A strong fundraising process starts with three things.

One

A compelling story

Your product, traction and market turned into a narrative an investor can follow.

Two

The right investor targets

Investors matched to your stage, sector and cheque size — not a generic list.

Three

Consistent outreach

Personalised messages and follow-ups, so no conversation goes cold.

Our goal.

To make fundraising more focused, structured, and easier for early-stage founders to navigate — so founders can spend less time figuring out fundraising and more time building their company.
